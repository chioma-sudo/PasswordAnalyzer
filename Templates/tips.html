PASSWORD TIPS

Create Stronger Passwords

Follow these simple practices to make your passwords harder to guess and safer to use.

01

Use a Longer Password

Aim for at least 8 characters, but preferably use a longer password or passphrase whenever possible.

02

Mix Different Character Types

Use a combination of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.

03

Avoid Common Passwords

Avoid passwords such as common words, simple number combinations, and other passwords that are easy to guess.

04

Avoid Repeated Patterns

Passwords containing repeated characters or obvious patterns can be easier to predict.

05

Avoid Personal Information

Do not use easily available information such as your name, birthday, phone number, or other personal details.

06

Don't Reuse Passwords

Use different passwords for important accounts so that one compromised password does not put multiple accounts at risk.

🛡️

REMEMBER

Strong passwords are only one part of security.

Where available, enable multi-factor authentication and use a trusted password manager to help manage unique passwords.
